# Privacy Policy — Lagom Lens

Lagom Lens does not collect, store, or transmit personal data to its developer.

**What it does:** When you hover a subtitle word (or, holding Shift, a whole
subtitle line), that text is sent directly from your browser to
[MyMemory](https://mymemory.translated.net/) for translation. When you save a
word, the subtitle line it appeared in is sent too, so it can be translated as
an example sentence. No other page content, browsing history, or personal
information is sent.

**Third-party services:** These requests are subject to MyMemory's own terms and
privacy policy:
- MyMemory: https://mymemory.translated.net/terms-and-conditions — submitted words
  may be stored and added to MyMemory's public translation memory.

**Local storage:** Your settings (enabled/disabled) are stored using the browser's
`storage.sync` API and are not accessible to the developer. Words you save, with
their translations, example sentences and the site they came from, are stored
only on your device with the browser's `storage.local` API. They leave it only
when you export them to a file yourself.

**No analytics, tracking, or ads.**
